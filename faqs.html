Basics

🏡 About Merahi

What is Merahi?
Merahi is a rural village in the Hasanpura block of Siwan district, Bihar, India, primarily an agricultural community with its own community-run website.
What is Merahi known for?
Its farmland, the riverside Chhath Ghat, several temples including Shiv Mandir, Ram Janki Durga Shiv Mandir and Maa Kali Mandir, a sacred Brahma Baba peepal tree, and its government primary school running since 1960.
Does Merahi have a Wikipedia page?
Yes — Merahi has its own Wikipedia article, linked from this site's About Village page.
Getting Here

📍 Location & How To Reach

Where is Merahi located?
At coordinates 26.422°N, 84.241°E, on the Gangetic plains of northern Bihar.
Which state, district and block is Merahi in?
Bihar state, Siwan district, Hasanpura block.
How far is Merahi from Siwan and Hasanpura?
About 7 km from Hasanpura, the block headquarters, and about 15 km from Siwan, the district headquarters.
How do I reach Merahi by road?
Merahi is connected by road from both Hasanpura and Siwan. Buses and shared or private vehicles run to Hasanpura, from where local transport covers the last stretch to the village.
What is the nearest railway station to Merahi?
Duraundha Junction is the closest railway station to the Hasanpura area, about 11 km away. Siwan Junction (station code SV), about 15–16 km away, is the larger, better-connected station with direct trains from cities such as Lucknow, Kanpur, Delhi and Kolkata.
What is the nearest airport to Merahi?
Patna Airport, roughly 100 km away, is the nearest major airport.
Demographics

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Population & People

What is the population of Merahi?
1,829 as per the 2011 Census of India — 819 men and 1,010 women.
How many households are in Merahi?
291 households, with an average household size of around 6 people.
What is the sex ratio in Merahi?
About 1,233 females per 1,000 males — notably higher than Bihar's state average.
How many children live in Merahi?
319 children aged 0–6, making up about 17% of the village's population.
What is the literacy rate in Merahi?
63.04% of the total population (566 literate men, 587 literate women). Calculated as an "effective" literacy rate excluding children under 7 — the standard census method — this rises to around 76%.
Economy

🌾 Work & Livelihood

What do people in Merahi do for a living?
Agriculture is the main occupation. The village and its surrounding area also have a small health centre, a panchayat office, and grocery shops providing local services.
How many people in Merahi are employed?
Per the 2011 Census, 398 residents were recorded as workers. Of these, only 30 had "main work" — steady employment or earning for 6+ months a year — while the remaining 368 were "marginal workers", meaning seasonal or short-term work, reflecting the village's dependence on farming cycles. The rest of the population — children, students, homemakers, the elderly and others — fall under the broader census category of "non-workers", which is not the same as being unemployed.
Is farming the main occupation in Merahi?
Yes — agriculture is by far the most common livelihood in Merahi, growing wheat, paddy and seasonal vegetables.
Schooling

📚 Education

Is there a school in Merahi?
Yes, a government primary school — Rajkiya Prathmik Vidyalaya Merahi — established in 1960.
What classes does the school teach and in what medium?
Classes 1 to 5, co-educational, Hindi medium, run by Bihar's Education Department.
Where do children in Merahi go to school after Class 5?
Most move on to schools in Hasanpura or Siwan for higher classes.
Local Government

🏛️ Governance & Administration

How is Merahi governed?
Through Bihar's Panchayati Raj system, like most villages in the state.
Which panchayat does Merahi belong to?
Pakari Gram Panchayat, within Hasanpura block.
Who is the mukhiya of Merahi's panchayat?
Rita Devi currently serves as mukhiya of Pakari panchayat.
Which assembly and Lok Sabha constituency is Merahi part of?
Daraunda assembly (Vidhan Sabha) constituency and Siwan parliamentary (Lok Sabha) constituency.
Climate

🌦️ Weather & Best Time To Visit

What is the climate like in Merahi?
Humid subtropical (Köppen classification Cwa) — hot summers, a monsoon season from roughly late June to September, and cool, dry winters.
When is the best time to visit Merahi?
October to March, during the cooler, drier months, is generally the most comfortable time to visit.
Can I check live weather for Merahi?
Yes — this site's Weather page shows live conditions, the past week, and a week-ahead forecast for Merahi.
Certificates & Schemes

📋 Government Services

How do I apply for a residence certificate in Merahi?
Through Bihar's RTPS portal at serviceonline.bihar.gov.in, or at the Pakari panchayat office. RTPS Bihar toll-free: 1800-345-6345.
How do I apply for an income certificate in Merahi?
The same RTPS portal or panchayat office process used for residence certificates also covers income certificates.
How do I apply for a caste certificate in Merahi?
The same RTPS portal or panchayat office process used for residence certificates also covers caste certificates.
Where do I apply for MGNREGA, pension or housing scheme benefits?
At the Pakari panchayat office, which handles MGNREGA job cards, old-age/widow/disability pension applications, and PM Awas Yojana (Gramin) housing scheme applications.
What are the important helpline numbers for Merahi residents?
RTPS Bihar 1800-345-6345 (certificates), Lok Shikayat 1800-345-6284 (civic grievances), Ambulance 108, National Emergency 112, and the Siwan District Health Helpline 06154-242782.
