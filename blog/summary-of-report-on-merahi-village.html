Summary of Report on Merahi Village, Siwan, Bihar, India

 1. Merahi Village is in Siwan district, Bihar, India; population is 1829 with a female majority.

2. Agriculture is a key economic activity; local businesses are growing.

3. Improved roads, electricity, and digital connectivity are present.

4. The village has numerous temples and observes regional festivals.

5. The 2011 Census shows 1829 people; 819 males and 1010 females.

6. Literacy rate is approximately 63%; female literacy is slightly higher than male.

7. New shops and stores have opened, indicating economic diversification. 

8. A primary school and a coaching center provide educational services.

9. Improved roads and reliable electricity, supplemented by solar power, are present.

10. A community center facilitates social interaction and village development.
