Good To Know

❓ Merahi Weather — FAQ

🌦️
What is Merahi's climate like?
Merahi lies in Siwan district on Bihar's Gangetic plains, which has a humid subtropical climate (Köppen Cwa) — hot summers, a monsoon season, and cool, dry winters.
🔥
How hot does it get in Merahi in summer?
Summers, from April to June, are hot, with daytime temperatures commonly reaching 38–44°C, especially in May and June. Hot, dry "Loo" winds are common in this part of Bihar during this period.
🌧️
When is the monsoon season in Merahi?
The monsoon usually arrives around late June and lasts through September, with July typically bringing the heaviest rainfall.
❄️
How cold does it get in Merahi in winter?
Winters, from November to mid-March, are cool and pleasant. January is the coldest month — nighttime lows can drop to around 5°C, among the coolest in Bihar, though daytime temperatures usually stay in the high teens to low 20s°C.
🗓️
What is the best time to visit Merahi?
October to March, during the cooler, drier months, is generally the most comfortable time to visit.

Merahi is a medium-sized, rural village located in the Hasanpura block of the Siwan district in Bihar, India. Primarily an agricultural community, it spans about 258.8 hectares and is home to nearly 1,830 residents.
