About Merahi

Merahi Village represents the beauty of rural India with green farms, traditional homes, cultural harmony, eco-friendly living, and strong community values. The village preserves its old heritage while embracing sustainable development.

Our Mission

Our mission is to preserve the cultural heritage and agricultural strength of Merahi Village while creating better opportunities in education, healthcare, sustainability, animal husbandry, youth development, and rural innovation.

Our Vision

To make Merahi Village a model smart village where agriculture, education, healthcare, environmental conservation, and cultural heritage grow together for future generations.
