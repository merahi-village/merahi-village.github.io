Braham Baba

Braham Baba is essentially a peepal tree treated as sacred — a common sight at the edge of many North Indian villages, and Merahi is no exception. A related shrine of the same kind nearby is known as Brahmsthan. Villagers stop to pay their respects as they pass, making it a small but steady part of everyday life here.
