Chhath Ghat

Locally known as Meraha, Chhath Ghat is built around a large village pond that does a lot more than sit pretty — families use it to water cattle, irrigate nearby fields, and gather here each year for the rituals of Chhath Puja. It's a reminder that despite India's cities racing ahead, everyday life in villages like Merahi still runs on ponds, fields and festivals rather than concrete and traffic.

Merahi is a medium-sized, rural village located in the Hasanpura block of the Siwan district in Bihar, India. Primarily an agricultural community, it spans about 258.8 hectares and is home to nearly 1,830 residents.
