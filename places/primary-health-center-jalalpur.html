Primary Health Centre, Jalalpur

The Primary Health Centre (also run as a Community Health Centre) at Jalalpur, in the Gurujwa area, is the nearest government healthcare facility serving Merahi and the surrounding villages of Hasanpura block, Siwan district. For families in Merahi, it's usually the first stop for everyday medical care — well before a longer trip to the Sadar Hospital in Siwan town becomes necessary.

📍 Gurujwa, Jalalpur, Hasanpura 🏥 Government Health Facility 📮 PIN: 841240

🩺 What a Primary Health Centre Offers

Primary Health Centres (PHCs) and Community Health Centres (CHCs) are the backbone of India's rural healthcare system, forming the first proper medical touchpoint above the village-level sub-centre. In Bihar, facilities of this kind are typically staffed to provide:

🧑‍⚕️
Outpatient Care
General OPD consultations and basic treatment
🤰
Maternal Health
Antenatal & postnatal (ANC/PNC) checkups, safe deliveries
💉
Immunization
Routine child & maternal vaccination under the National Immunization Schedule
👨‍👩‍👧
Family Planning
Counselling and contraceptive services
🩹
First Aid & Minor Care
Treatment for minor injuries and common illnesses
💊
Essential Medicines
Basic diagnostics and free/subsidised generic medicines
🚑
Referral Services
Onward referral to Sadar Hospital, Siwan for specialist or emergency care
🏘️
Area Served
Merahi and surrounding villages of Hasanpura block

Exact services and staffing at any individual PHC/CHC can vary and change over time — the list above reflects what facilities of this type typically provide across Bihar's rural health network.
