Introduction

Welcome to the official website of Merahi Village, Hasanpura, Siwan, Bihar. This website is dedicated to preserving the heritage, culture, history, traditions, and development of our village.

Our mission is to preserve local identity while promoting education, community development, sustainability, and cultural awareness.

Our vision is to build a stronger, smarter, and more sustainable Merahi Village for future generations.

Explore More

Merahi is a medium-sized, rural village located in the Hasanpura block of the Siwan district in Bihar, India. Primarily an agricultural community, it spans about 258.8 hectares and is home to nearly 1,830 residents.
