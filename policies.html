📖 Introduction

Merahi Village is an independent community information platform created to share the village's history, culture, places, people and everyday life. It exists to share the village's history, culture, people and everyday life — not to run a business, sell anything, or profit from visitor data.

These policies explain how the site is built, what happens when you use the contact form, which outside tools power features like the map embeds and live weather, and what rights you have as a visitor. If anything here is unclear, the Contact page is the fastest way to ask.

🔒 Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy and do not sell, rent, or trade personal information for commercial purposes — ever. This site has no login system, no user accounts, and no advertising network of any kind.

  • Information you submit through the Contact form is used only to read and reply to your message.
  • We do not build advertising profiles, and no data collected here is sold to third parties.
  • Basic, aggregate visitor statistics (such as page views) may exist through GitHub Pages' own hosting logs — these are not personally identifying and are not used to track individuals.

🍪 Cookie Policy

This site does not set its own tracking or advertising cookies. Any cookies you encounter here come from the third-party services listed above — mainly YouTube, if you play an embedded video, and possibly Google Maps or Google Fonts as part of their normal delivery process. You can block or clear these at any time through your browser's settings without affecting your ability to read the site's content.

📜 Terms & Conditions

  • Use this website responsibly and for its intended purpose — learning about and celebrating Merahi village.
  • Do not publish, submit or attempt to inject false, harmful, or misleading information through the Contact form or any other channel.
  • Respect the village's values, culture and traditions in anything you send us.
  • Unauthorized attempts to access, modify, or disrupt this website or its hosting are prohibited.

✅ Content Policy

  • Content on this site is kept as accurate and respectful as we can make it.
  • Deliberate misinformation about the village, its people, or its history is not published.
  • Illegal, hateful, or harmful content has no place here and will be removed if it ever appears.
  • Content may be edited, corrected or removed at the administrator's discretion, including in response to feedback from villagers.

🤝 Community Guidelines

  • Treat everyone connected to this site and village with respect.
  • Keep discussion and feedback constructive.
  • Abusive, threatening, or discriminatory language will not be entertained in any message we receive.
  • The goal of this site is to support Merahi's development and community harmony — contributions in that spirit are always welcome.

🧒 Children's Privacy

If a parent or guardian believes a child has submitted personal information to us, please reach out through the Contact page and it will be removed.
