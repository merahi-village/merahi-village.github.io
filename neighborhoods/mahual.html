Mahual (Mahual Mahal)

Mahual — also known locally as Mahual Mahal — is a village near Merahi that falls under the administration of Siwan District, Government of Bihar. Unlike Merahi and most of its immediate neighbors, which sit within Hasanpura block, Mahual's local governance, land revenue records and welfare distribution are coordinated through the Daraundha Development Block, under the Maharajganj Sub-Division.
