Laheji

Laheji is one of the larger villages near Merahi, within the same Hasanpura block but governed by its own gram panchayat rather than Pakari. It's also home to Mandrauli, a smaller village that falls under Laheji's own panchayat and sits just south of Merahi.

Laheji has produced some notable residents, including politician Om Prakash Yadav and the Hindi writer and playwright Hrishikesh Sulabh, a recipient of the Sangeet Natak Akademi Award.
