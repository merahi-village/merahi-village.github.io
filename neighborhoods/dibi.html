Dibi

Dibi sits right alongside Merahi, sharing the same Pakari gram panchayat and Hasanpura block administration. It's one of the closest villages to Merahi, and the two communities are closely linked through everyday life, local governance and shared local roads — the Dibi-Merahi road, in fact, is the same road that runs past Brahma Baba shrine on Merahi's edge.

📍 Hasanpura block, Siwan district 🏘️ Pakari Gram Panchayat 📮 PIN: 841240

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Dibi at a Glance

As per the 2011 Census of India, Dibi recorded a population of 2,164 — 998 men and 1,166 women — spread across 156 households, on a total area of about 147.35 hectares.
