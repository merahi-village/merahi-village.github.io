Hasanpura

Hasanpura is the Community Development Block headquarters that Merahi belongs to — about 7 km away, it's where most of Merahi's residents go for anything beyond what the village itself offers: higher schooling, larger markets, and block-level government offices. Hasanpura has its own Nagar Panchayat (town council), and the wider Hasanpura block contains 13 gram panchayats, including Merahi's own Pakari and neighboring Laheji.

📋 Hasanpura Block at a Glance

The wider Hasanpura Community Development Block covers about 96.56 km² and recorded a population of 149,580 across its 13 gram panchayats, per the 2011 Census of India.
