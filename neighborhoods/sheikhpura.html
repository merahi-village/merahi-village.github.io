Sheikhpura

Sheikhpura lies about 3 km from the Hasanpura block headquarters, within the same block as Merahi. Unlike most of its neighbors, Sheikhpura falls under the Raghunathpur assembly constituency rather than Daraunda, though it shares the same Siwan Lok Sabha constituency and Saran administrative division as the rest of the block.
