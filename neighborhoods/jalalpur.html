Jalalpur

Jalalpur is a village near Merahi in Hasanpura block, and it's a name Merahi residents already know well — the area's nearest government healthcare facility, the Primary Health Centre, Jalalpur, is located here, serving Merahi and the surrounding villages.

📍 Hasanpura block, Siwan district 🏥 Home to the area's Health Centre 📮 PIN: 841240

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Jalalpur at a Glance

As per the 2011 Census of India, Jalalpur recorded a population of 1,443 — 732 men and 711 women — across 210 households, with a literacy rate of around 67.34%.
