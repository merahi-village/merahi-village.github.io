Mandrauli

Mandrauli is about as close a neighbor as Merahi has — sitting roughly 1.25 km to the south, on the same side of Hasanpura block as Pakari. It falls under the Laheji gram panchayat rather than Pakari, but day-to-day, residents of both villages regularly cross paths given how close they are.
