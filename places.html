Landmarks & Community Spots

Places in Merahi

From riverside ghats to roadside temples, these are the spots that anchor everyday life in the village. Tap any card to read more, see photos and find it on the map.

Riverside

🌅 Chhath Ghat

The village's large pond and gathering point for Chhath Puja — also known as Meraha.
Temple

🛕 Shiv Mandir

Merahi's oldest temple, standing on Sharmaji Chowk Road at the heart of the village.
Temple

🛕 Ram Janki Durga Shiv Mandir

A landmark temple at the village entrance, host to two of Merahi's biggest annual fairs.
Sacred Site

🌳 Brahma Baba

A worshipped peepal tree — a small but steady part of village life at two spots nearby.
Education

🏫 Primary School

The government school in Merahi, teaching Classes 1–5 since 1960.
Healthcare

🏥 Health Centre, Jalalpur

The nearest government healthcare facility serving Merahi and Hasanpura block.
Temple

🛕 Maa Kali Mandir

A peaceful temple on Hasanpura Road with an open field where children play.
Governance

🏛️ Pakari Panchayat

The gram panchayat Merahi falls under — mukhiya, services and helpline numbers.

Merahi is a medium-sized, rural village located in the Hasanpura block of the Siwan district in Bihar, India. Primarily an agricultural community, it spans about 258.8 hectares and is home to nearly 1,830 residents.
